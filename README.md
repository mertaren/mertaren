# Hi, I'm Mert 👋

I am a **Statistics** student at Gazi University, Ankara — specializing in **GNC (Guidance, Navigation & Control)** and **Autonomous UAV Systems**. I combine a strong mathematical background with hands-on experience in drone software, state estimation, and AI-based perception.

I am currently working on projects involving autonomous flight systems, sensor fusion, and real-time computer vision for UAVs.

---

### 🎯 Focus Area

**GNC & Autonomous UAV Systems**
Trajectory planning, control algorithms (PID, MPC), 6-DOF flight dynamics, and MAVLink-based drone communication.

**State Estimation & Sensor Fusion**
Kalman filtering (EKF/UKF), IMU + GPS fusion, and SLAM for GPS-denied environments.

**Computer Vision for UAVs**
Real-time object detection with YOLO, visual odometry, and image segmentation for aerial perception.

**Simulation & Embedded Deployment**
ROS2 + Gazebo-based UAV simulation pipelines and model deployment on embedded hardware (Jetson, Raspberry Pi).
